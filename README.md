# Juris.Track Landing

Create a responsive landing page for "Juris.Track", a SaaS platform that helps solo lawyers and small law firms track legal processes and communicate more easily with clients.

VISUAL STYLE:

- Color palette: deep navy blue (#0F1F3D or similar) as primary, warm amber/gold (#D4A94C or similar) as accent, white and light gray (#F5F6F8) for backgrounds

- Typography: a light serif font for headings (conveys legal trust and seriousness), clean sans-serif for body text (conveys modernity)

- Style: clean, professional, generous white space, thin line icons, subtle shadows on cards, rounded corners (not too sharp, not too playful)

- Overall feeling: security, organization, relief from chaos

SECTIONS TO INCLUDE:

1. HERO SECTION

- Headline: "Nunca mais perca um prazo processual" (or similar, in Portuguese)

- Subheadline: explain that Juris.Track connects directly to PJe and keeps lawyer and client informed automatically via WhatsApp

- Primary CTA button: "Quero testar gratuitamente" (amber/gold button, high contrast)

- Include a visual mockup placeholder (dashboard-style illustration or abstract graphic representing a process timeline/checklist) on the right side

2. PROBLEM SECTION

- Headline like: "Advogado autônomo não pode viver apagando incêndio"

- 3 short pain points with icons:

  - Prazos perdidos por falta de acompanhamento manual

  - Documentos e andamentos processuais espalhados e desorganizados

  - Clientes ansiosos mandando mensagem toda hora perguntando sobre o processo

3. HOW IT WORKS (3 steps, horizontal or vertical stepper with icons/numbers)

- Step 1: "Conecte seus processos" — integração direta com a API do PJe para importar automaticamente os andamentos

- Step 2: "Organize com check-lists" — o advogado marca etapas e prazos de cada processo, com lembretes automáticos

- Step 3: "Comunique-se sem esforço" — atualizações e prazos são enviados automaticamente para o cliente via WhatsApp

4. BENEFITS SECTION (grid of 3-4 cards with icons)

- Nunca perca um prazo — alertas automáticos

- Cliente informado sem esforço manual

- Tudo centralizado em um só lugar

- Menos tempo administrativo, mais tempo advogando

5. EMAIL CAPTURE FORM SECTION

- Headline: "Seja um dos primeiros a testar"

- Simple email input + CTA button ("Quero ser avisado")

- Small reassurance text below the form (e.g., "Sem spam, só novidades sobre o lançamento")

6. FOOTER

- Simple footer with logo/name "Juris.Track", a tagline, and placeholder social links

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:

- Fully responsive (mobile-first, works well on small screens)

- Smooth scroll and subtle fade-in animations on scroll for sections

- Use accessible color contrast

- All text in Brazilian Portuguese

- Make the email form functional-looking but it doesn't need to submit to a real backend (mock/dummy submission is fine for this prototype)
